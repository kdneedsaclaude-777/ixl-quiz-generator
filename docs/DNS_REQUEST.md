# DNS change request — email sending for conceptmastery.ca (QuizSpark)

**For:** whoever manages DNS for `conceptmastery.ca`
**Purpose:** let the QuizSpark app send account emails (sign-up verification, notifications) from a `@conceptmastery.ca` address via Resend (our email provider).
**Impact:** **additive and safe.** These records only *add* email-sending authentication. They do **not** change your website, and they do **not** affect your existing inboxes (e.g. `admin@conceptmastery.ca`) — the sending records sit on a dedicated `send.` subdomain.

---

## Records to add

> ⚠️ The exact values below are **generated by Resend** when the domain is added
> to our Resend account. The table shows the shape and purpose so you can
> pre-approve; the final values will be the ones Resend displays (we'll paste the
> exact strings).

| # | Type | Host / Name | Value (from Resend) | Purpose |
|---|------|-------------|---------------------|---------|
| 1 | **MX** | `send` | `feedback-smtp.<region>.amazonses.com` (priority 10) | Handles bounce/return-path for sent mail |
| 2 | **TXT** | `send` | `v=spf1 include:amazonses.com ~all` | SPF — authorizes Resend/SES to send |
| 3 | **TXT** | `resend._domainkey` | `p=<long DKIM public key>` | DKIM — cryptographically signs our mail |
| 4 | **TXT** *(optional)* | `_dmarc` | `v=DMARC1; p=none;` | DMARC — reporting policy (recommended) |

Notes for the DNS admin:
- All records are **add-only** — nothing existing is modified or removed.
- Records 1 & 2 are on the **`send` subdomain**, so they don't touch your main
  domain's existing SPF/MX (your normal email keeps working).
- If a root `_dmarc` already exists, **keep the existing one** — don't duplicate.
- Fully **reversible**: deleting these records later cleanly disables app email.

## After the records are added
We click **Verify** in Resend; once it goes green (minutes to a few hours), the
app sends from `no-reply@conceptmastery.ca`. No further action needed from the
DNS admin.
