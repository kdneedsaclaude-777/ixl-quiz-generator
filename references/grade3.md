# Grade 3 Math — IXL Ontario Curriculum

## A. Numbers and place value
1. Place value models up to thousands
2. Identify a digit's place value
3. Convert between standard and expanded form
4. Convert between place values
5. Write numbers in words up to 1000
6. Spell word names for numbers
7. Roman numerals I, V, X
8. Even or odd

## B. Addition
1. Add two-digit numbers
2. Add three-digit numbers
3. Add three or more numbers
4. Addition word problems
5. Complete the addition sentence
6. Estimate sums
7. Properties of addition

## C. Subtraction
1. Subtract two-digit numbers
2. Subtract three-digit numbers
3. Subtraction word problems
4. Complete the subtraction sentence
5. Estimate differences
6. Subtraction patterns over increasing place values

## D. Understand multiplication
1. Count equal groups
2. Identify multiplication expressions for equal groups
3. Write multiplication sentences for arrays
4. Relate addition and multiplication

## E. Multiplication
1. Multiplication facts up to 10
2. Multiplication facts up to 12
3. Multiply by a multiple of ten
4. Multiplication word problems
5. Complete the multiplication sentence
6. Properties of multiplication
7. Multiply three numbers

## F. Understand division
1. Divide by counting equal groups
2. Write division sentences for groups
3. Relate multiplication and division

## G. Division
1. Division facts up to 10
2. Division facts up to 12
3. Division word problems
4. Complete the division sentence
5. Divide with remainders

## H. Mixed operations
1. Add, subtract, multiply and divide
2. Multi-step word problems
3. Fact families: multiplication and division
4. Which sign makes the number sentence true

## I. Fractions
1. Identify fractions
2. Fractions of a whole
3. Fractions on number lines
4. Equivalent fractions
5. Compare fractions

## J. Money
1. Count money amounts
2. Add and subtract money amounts
3. Making change
4. Price lists

## K. Measurement
1. Measure length to the nearest centimetre
2. Metric units of length
3. Metric units of mass
4. Metric units of capacity
5. Perimeter
6. Area of figures made of unit squares

## L. Time
1. Read clocks and write times
2. Elapsed time
3. Convert time units
4. Time word problems

## M. Geometry
1. Identify and classify two-dimensional shapes
2. Count vertices, edges and faces
3. Identify three-dimensional figures
4. Lines, line segments and rays
5. Angles greater than, less than or equal to a right angle
6. Symmetry

## N. Data and graphs
1. Interpret bar graphs
2. Interpret pictographs
3. Interpret line plots
4. Create bar graphs
5. Frequency tables
