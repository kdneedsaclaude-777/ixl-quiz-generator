# Grade 2 Math — IXL Ontario Curriculum

## A. Counting and number patterns
1. Skip-count by twos, fives and tens
2. Skip-count by hundreds
3. Counting patterns up to 100
4. Counting patterns up to 1000
5. Number lines up to 100
6. Even or odd
7. Even or odd: counting objects
8. Ordinal numbers up to twentieth

## B. Place value
1. Place value models up to hundreds
2. Identify a digit's place value
3. Convert between standard and expanded form
4. Convert between place values
5. Write numbers in words up to 100
6. Regroup tens and ones

## C. Comparing and ordering
1. Comparing numbers up to 100
2. Comparing numbers up to 1000
3. Put numbers up to 100 in order
4. Put numbers up to 1000 in order
5. Greatest and least: up to 1000

## D. Addition
1. Addition facts up to 20
2. Add two-digit numbers without regrouping
3. Add two-digit numbers with regrouping
4. Add three numbers up to two digits
5. Addition word problems up to two digits
6. Complete the addition sentence
7. Balance addition equations
8. Add three-digit numbers

## E. Subtraction
1. Subtraction facts up to 20
2. Subtract two-digit numbers without regrouping
3. Subtract two-digit numbers with regrouping
4. Subtraction word problems up to two digits
5. Complete the subtraction sentence
6. Balance subtraction equations
7. Subtract three-digit numbers

## F. Mixed operations
1. Add and subtract numbers up to 20
2. Addition and subtraction word problems
3. Which sign makes the number sentence true
4. Fact families

## G. Multiplication
1. Count equal groups
2. Identify multiplication expressions for equal groups
3. Write multiplication sentences for equal groups
4. Multiplication facts for 2, 5 and 10

## H. Money
1. Count coins and bills
2. Compare money amounts
3. Add and subtract money amounts
4. Making change

## I. Measurement
1. Measure using a ruler: centimetres
2. Measure using a ruler: inches
3. Compare and convert metric units of length
4. Read a thermometer
5. Choose the appropriate measuring tool

## J. Time
1. Match analogue and digital clocks
2. Read clocks and write times
3. A.M. or P.M.
4. Elapsed time
5. Days, weeks and months

## K. Geometry
1. Identify two-dimensional shapes
2. Count sides and vertices
3. Identify three-dimensional figures
4. Identify faces of three-dimensional figures
5. Symmetry
6. Equal parts: halves, thirds and quarters

## L. Data and graphs
1. Interpret picture graphs
2. Interpret bar graphs
3. Interpret tally charts
4. Create picture graphs
