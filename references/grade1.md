# Grade 1 Math — IXL Ontario Curriculum

## A. Counting and number patterns
1. Count to 10
2. Count to 20
3. Count to 50
4. Count to 100
5. Counting on the hundred chart
6. Skip-count by twos
7. Skip-count by fives
8. Skip-count by tens
9. Identify numbers as even or odd
10. Number lines up to 20
11. Ordinal numbers up to tenth
12. Writing numbers in words up to 20

## B. Comparing and ordering
1. Comparing numbers up to 10
2. Comparing numbers up to 20
3. Comparing numbers up to 100
4. Put numbers up to 20 in order
5. Put numbers up to 100 in order
6. Greatest and least
7. More, fewer and equal groups

## C. Addition up to 20
1. Add with pictures up to 5
2. Add with pictures up to 10
3. Addition facts up to 5
4. Addition facts up to 10
5. Addition facts up to 20
6. Add doubles
7. Add three numbers up to 10
8. Make a number using addition
9. Addition word problems up to 10
10. Complete the addition sentence up to 10

## D. Subtraction up to 20
1. Subtract with pictures up to 5
2. Subtract with pictures up to 10
3. Subtraction facts up to 5
4. Subtraction facts up to 10
5. Subtraction facts up to 20
6. Subtract doubles
7. Subtraction word problems up to 10
8. Complete the subtraction sentence up to 10

## E. Place value
1. Tens and ones
2. Identify a digit's place value
3. Make a number using tens and ones
4. Place value models up to 20
5. Place value models up to 100

## F. Money
1. Identify coins
2. Value of a coin
3. Count pennies, nickels and dimes
4. Compare money amounts

## G. Measurement
1. Long and short
2. Tall and short
3. Light and heavy
4. Holds more or less
5. Compare size, weight and capacity

## H. Time
1. Match clocks and times
2. Read clocks and write times: hour
3. Read clocks and write times: half hour
4. Days of the week
5. Months of the year

## I. Geometry
1. Name the two-dimensional shape
2. Name the three-dimensional shape
3. Count sides and corners
4. Equal parts
5. Identify halves and quarters

## J. Data and graphs
1. Read a picture graph
2. Read a tally chart
3. Which picture is most or least
